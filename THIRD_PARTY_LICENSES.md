## Background images (`public/assets/img/`)

- Generated by the team for this project with **Higgsfield** (GPT Image 2.6 model), and used subject to Higgsfield's terms of service.
- Each image has a visible "SI-generated" watermark burned in, as the NASA Space Apps rules require for AI-generated images.
- No NASA logos, flags, mission patches or identifiers, no people, and no real spacecraft appear in them.

## NASA and space-agency resources

NASA resources are **linked and cited, not copied**. The full list, and what each one shaped, is in the in-game Mission Library (`/sources.php`) and in `database/seed.sql` (`reference_sources`). The 13 short NASA fact statements in the game are paraphrased from those sources, each with a link.

## Fonts

The game uses each device's built-in system fonts; no font files are bundled.
